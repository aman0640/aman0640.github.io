# Privacy Policy for Warranty Tracker

**Effective Date:** November 6, 2026

A-Man Games ("we," "us," or "our") built the Warranty Tracker app. This SERVICE is provided by A-Man Games and is intended for use as is.

## 1. Information Collection and Use
The core functionality of Warranty Tracker operates offline. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any of your personal warranty information or product details. All data entered into the app (such as product names, purchase dates, and warranty periods) is stored strictly locally on your device.

## 2. Internet Connection
While your warranty data remains completely offline, the app requires an internet connection strictly to display advertisements and to process in-app subscriptions. Your personally entered data is never uploaded to any servers, cloud storage, or external databases.

## 3. Device Permissions
The app may request the following permissions to function properly:
- **Storage:** Solely for the purpose of saving and retrieving your warranty tracking data locally on your device.
- **Notifications:** To send you local alerts and reminders when a product's warranty is approaching expiration.

## 4. Advertisements and Subscriptions (Third-Party Services)
To support the developer, the app contains advertisements. We also offer an optional in-app subscription to remove these ads for an ad-free experience.
- **Ad Networks:** We use third-party ad networks (such as Google AdMob) to serve ads. These third-party services may collect certain anonymous technical data, such as your device's Advertising ID, to provide relevant ads.
- **In-App Purchases:** Subscriptions are securely processed entirely through Google Play Billing. We do not collect or have access to your financial or payment information.

## 5. Data Security
We value your trust in using our app. Since your warranty data remains on your device, the security of this data is inherently tied to the security of your device itself. We recommend protecting your device with a passcode or biometric lock.

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be updated within the app or on our developer page.
