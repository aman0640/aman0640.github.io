Wordcraft: Daily Puzzle

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application Wordcraft: Daily Puzzle, created by Aman Kumar (operating as A-Man Games). This policy governs the manner in which the Application handles data, user privacy, and third-party interactions.

Information Collection and Use

The Application is designed to offer a seamless offline gameplay experience.

  • No Account Required: You are not required to create an account, register, or log in to use the Application.
  • Offline Playability: The Application can be played entirely offline without an active internet connection.
  • No Direct Data Collection: The Service Provider does not directly collect, store, transmit, or share any Personally Identifiable Information (PII).

Any game progress, settings, or preferences are stored locally on your device's internal storage and are not transmitted to servers managed by the Service Provider.

Third-Party Services

The Application integrates third-party software development kits (SDKs) and services to facilitate game functionality, display advertisements, and process optional transactions. The third-party service providers include Google Play Services, Google AdMob, and Google Play Billing.

Subscriptions, Payments, and Security

The Application offers optional subscriptions and in-app purchases processed through Google Play Billing. The Service Provider does not store, process, or have access to your payment details. Because the Application does not collect or store personal data directly on external servers, the risk of personal data exposure through the Service Provider is minimized.

Children's Privacy (13+)

The Application does not address anyone under the age of 13. The Service Provider does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 years of age.
